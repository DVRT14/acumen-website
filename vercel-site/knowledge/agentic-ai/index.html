WHAT IS AGENTIC AI

Not a smarter chatbot. A colleague that finishes the task.

Most companies already use AI to answer questions or draft content. Agentic AI goes further. It plans multi-step work, uses your tools and systems on its own, and sees a task through to the end, checking in with a person only where that matters.

CHATBOT

Answers a question when you ask it. A person still does the work that follows.

AGENT

Reads the incoming request, checks your systems, prepares the outcome, and hands off only the exception to a person.

In practice, an agent can:

Read and understand documents

Extract the relevant fields from invoices, contracts, forms, drawings, or emails, whatever the format.

Enter and update data in your systems

Fill in and update records directly in your ERP, CRM, or internal tools, no manual copy-paste.

Make decisions within clear rules

Approve, flag, or escalate a case based on the criteria you define, consistently, every time.

Connect and act across systems

Look something up in one system, check it against another, and complete the action in a third.

Watch for triggers and follow through

Pick up a new email, order, or ticket the moment it arrives and start the process automatically.

Keep a person informed and in control

Notify the right person, ask for approval, or hand off the exception, so nothing happens unsupervised where it shouldn’t.

Frequently asked questions

What is agentic AI?

Agentic AI plans a multi-step task, works through it using your own tools and systems, and completes it on its own — checking in with a person only where that matters, rather than just answering a question like a chatbot.

How is agentic AI different from a chatbot?

A chatbot answers a question and a person still does the work that follows. An agent reads the incoming request, checks your systems, prepares the outcome, and hands off only the exception to a person.

How widely is agentic AI actually used today?

88% of companies already use AI in at least one part of the business (McKinsey, 2025), but fewer than 10% have scaled an AI agent far enough to deliver measurable value (McKinsey, 2026). Gartner projects 40% of enterprise applications will include task-specific AI agents by the end of 2026.

What kind of tasks can an agentic AI system handle?

Document intake and classification (invoices, contracts, forms), quoting and pricing, customer and support ticket routing, and cross-system decisions that pull data from ERP, CRM, and other tools.

How does Acumen build an agentic AI system?

Acumen follows a three-step process: identify the right use case and check the underlying data is ready, build a scoped end-to-end proof of concept, then integrate the proven agent into existing systems and scale to the next use case.
