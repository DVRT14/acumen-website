Your team spends hours on work an agent could handle. We build the agent.

## What is agentic AI

Not a smarter chatbot. A colleague that finishes the task.

Most companies already use AI to answer questions or draft content. Agentic AI goes further. It plans multi-step work, uses your tools and systems on its own, and sees a task through to the end, checking in with a person only where that matters.

|         | What it does |
| ------- | ------------ |
| Chatbot | Answers a question when you ask it. A person still does the work that follows. |
| Agent   | Reads the incoming request, checks your systems, prepares the outcome, and hands off only the exception to a person. |

### In practice, an agent can:

-   **Read and understand documents:** extract the relevant fields from invoices, contracts, forms, drawings, or emails, whatever the format.
-   **Enter and update data in your systems:** fill in and update records directly in your ERP, CRM, or internal tools, no manual copy-paste.
-   **Make decisions within clear rules:** approve, flag, or escalate a case based on the criteria you define, consistently, every time.
-   **Connect and act across systems:** look something up in one system, check it against another, and complete the action in a third.
-   **Watch for triggers and follow through:** pick up a new email, order, or ticket the moment it arrives and start the process automatically.
-   **Keep a person informed and in control:** notify the right person, ask for approval, or hand off the exception, so nothing happens unsupervised where it shouldn’t.

## The added value

Why this earns a place in your business, not just your innovation budget:

-   **Speed:** work that took half a day happens in minutes, while your team moves on to the next thing.
-   **Consistency:** the same steps, correctly, every time. No fatigue, no shortcuts under deadline pressure.
-   **Availability:** agents don’t wait for office hours. Requests get handled nights, weekends, and everything in between.
-   **Focus:** less time on repetitive steps means more time for the judgment calls that actually need a person.

## Why now

The gap between testing and using: most organisations aren’t short on AI experiments. They’re short on AI that runs, unsupervised, every single day.

-   **88%** of companies already use AI in at least one part of the business (McKinsey, 2025).
-   **Fewer than 10%** have scaled an AI agent far enough to deliver measurable value (McKinsey, 2026).
-   **40%** of enterprise applications will include task-specific AI agents by end of 2026 (Gartner).

## What it means for your business

### Document intake

Manual document processing eats your team’s time. We build agents that read, classify, and route documents automatically, with a person checking only the exceptions. For example:

-   Supplier fiches and safety data sheets
-   Invoices and purchase requests
-   Contracts and compliance forms

### Quoting and pricing

Slow, inconsistent quotes lose deals. We build agents that pull the right data, apply your pricing logic, and prepare a quote automatically, in minutes, not days. For example:

-   Margin calculation
-   Configurable product quotes
-   Approval routing

### Customer and support requests

Support tickets pile up before the right person sees them. We build agents that categorise and route incoming requests instantly, day or night. For example:

-   Ticket classification
-   First-line responses
-   Escalation to the right team

### Cross-system decisions

Good decisions get delayed because data lives in five systems. We build agents that connect those systems and surface exactly what a person needs to decide. For example:

-   ERP and CRM lookups
-   Inventory and supplier checks
-   Consolidated reporting

Every one of these starts small, proves value on one process, and expands from there. That’s deliberate: agentic AI works best as a series of contained wins, not one big transformation project.
