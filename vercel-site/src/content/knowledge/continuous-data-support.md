At Acumen, our commitment doesn’t stop at go-live. A data platform, a set of reports or a planning model is not a stationary piece of software: it keeps changing as the business and the systems around it change. Continuous data support keeps it reliable, relevant and used.

## Why support matters after implementation

Once a solution is implemented, its real life begins. Data sources change, new KPIs are needed, users come and go, and the volume of data keeps growing. Without care, trust in the numbers erodes: a failed load goes unnoticed, a report shows yesterday’s figures, or a new team never learns how to use the dashboards.

Quality support and maintenance protect the investment you made. They make sure the data can be trusted, stays relevant, and that users know how to get the most out of it.

## What continuous data support covers

We call our approach **Continued Care**: the right mix of services for your environment and your users. Typical services include:

- **Monitoring:** automated checks on system health and availability, detection of errors in data loads or report runs, and checks on functional KPIs so the data stays correct and complete.
- **Incident management:** resolving issues found by monitoring or reported by users, such as failed data pipelines, report execution problems or data inconsistencies.
- **User support:** from service desk tasks to onboarding and training new users, managing security and answering ad hoc questions.
- **Change management:** adding new KPIs, adapting data models, handling changes in data sources and building new dashboards.
- **Auditing:** reviewing the use of dashboards, reports and schedules, monitoring costs and usage, and checking licence compliance.
- **Maintenance:** installing fixes and new versions, testing backups, tuning performance, cleaning up unused objects and keeping documentation current.

> A data solution only keeps delivering value when someone keeps caring for it.

## Specialists, not a generic IT provider

Supporting a data environment is different from supporting a generic application. It takes people who understand data pipelines, models and reporting, and who can tell a technical failure from a data quality issue. Acumen specialises in data solutions, so the people who support your environment understand how it was built and why.

## Tailored to your environment

No two environments are the same. We work with you to agree the service scope that fits your platform, your users and your internal team, from keeping daily operations running to onboarding new user groups.
