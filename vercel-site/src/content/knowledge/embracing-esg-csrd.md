In the wake of global challenges such as climate change, social inequality, and corporate governance failures, Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles have emerged as vital metrics for evaluating the sustainability and societal impact of businesses. Driven by national and intranational institutions, regulations are being reviewed, extended or introduced. This whitepaper seeks to provide an insight in how companies can manage this increased regulatory attention from a technological perspective, with a specific focus on the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD).
