Spaas Candle’s has been in operation since 1853, specializing in crafting candles. Their aim is to captivate and inspire people with their products. Thanks to Power BI they can always rely on having the most accurate information at their fingertips.

## How we supported Spaas

**Before**

For Spaas to visualize their data, they had to manually extract data from SAP and import it into Excel on a monthly basis. This process required labor-intensive manual transformations, taking up significant time and resources. As a result, the generated reports were slow-moving and inflexible, providing little room for customization. Furthermore, the manual nature of these tasks frequently resulted in errors.

**The journey**

We began by conducting a thorough analysis of the existing reports and underlying data, which we then structured into a star schema. This schema serves as a flexible foundation upon which to build our reporting framework. Following our analysis, we established a pipeline via ADF (Azure Data Factory) for nightly ingestion of data from SAP into our data warehouse. Once this data was curated and validated, we initiated the construction of Power BI dashboards and reports. Throughout this process, we prioritized listening to our clients’ specific objectives, ensuring that the reports delivered precisely the insights necessary for the company’s growth.

## Challenges and benefits

**Challenges**

-   Manual labour
-   Static reports

**Benefits**

With this reporting system, they have the autonomy to select the insights they wish to gain: whether it’s customer turnover, product margins, or any other metrics. The possibilities are limitless.

**Updated Daily**

Thanks to the daily updates, they can always rely on having the most accurate information at their fingertips.
